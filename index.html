Generate a 7-week, 8-team round robin

Choose exactly 8 confirmed teams for one night and court. The generator shuffles the matchups each week and automatically assigns the league time slots. Monday through Friday: 6:30 PM, 7:30 PM, 8:30 PM, and 9:30 PM. Sunday: 5:30 PM, 6:30 PM, 7:30 PM, and 8:30 PM. It creates 4 matches per week for 7 weeks, so every team plays every other team once.
