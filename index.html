Build the league schedule

The league runs in 7-week sessions. Choose a night and court to see all approved teams for that slot. Select one or more teams, enter a different game time and opponent for each, and save them together. Captains will see scheduled games in their account.

Generate a 7-week, 8-team round robin

Choose exactly 8 confirmed teams for one night and court. The generator shuffles the matchups each week and automatically assigns the league time slots. Monday through Friday: 6:30 PM, 7:30 PM, 8:30 PM, and 9:30 PM. Sunday: 5:30 PM, 6:30 PM, 7:30 PM, and 8:30 PM. It creates 4 matches per week for 7 weeks, so every team plays every other team once.

Import teams from Excel

Upload an .xlsx, .xls, or .csv spreadsheet. Review the preview before importing. Imported teams are added as team registration records and will appear in Teams & rosters and All registrations.

Recommended columns: Team Name, Captain Name, Captain Email, Captain Phone, Location, Game Day, Court, Division, Team Type, Session Name, Status, Roster. Roster can be teammate names separated by commas or semicolons. Column names are flexible; unmatched fields can be left blank.

Importing does not delete existing records. Check your preview carefully to avoid duplicates.
